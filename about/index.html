Research background

My research interests are brain–computer interfaces and human–robot interaction. I am interested in using neural, physiological, and behavioral signals to help interactive systems adapt to users’ intentions and states.

I am a research assistant in the Brain–Computer Interface Lab at Academia Sinica, working with Dr. Yu-Te Wang. My experience includes EEG experiments and collaborative analysis, VR development, and mechanical design for assistive and biomedical systems.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
