#LLM#ReinforcementLearning

LLM-driven robot pet

I developed a MuJoCo robot pet that combines VLM-derived human behavioral cues with simulated internal states to guide LLM-based behavior selection. I also designed speed-dependent rewards for PPO locomotion and evaluated velocity tracking and stability across three training seeds.

Additional research & projects

#EEG#VR

VR motor rehabilitation with human and AI competition

BCI Lab, Academia Sinica
Jun 2026–present

A separate study using the RehabVerse platform to compare solo, human-opponent, and AI-opponent conditions during executed and imagined movements. I conducted experimental sessions and worked on EEG and EMG preprocessing, data-quality criteria, and ROI-based ERD analysis for hand and foot tasks.

#EEG#WearableDesign

Multichannel EEG Headset Development

BCI Lab, Academia Sinica
Jun 2026–present

I designed and fabricated a six-channel EEG headset for a planned ALS communication interface combining SSVEP-based selection and eye tracking.

I am also designing an EEG headset for use with AR glasses to record central and occipital activity, with a rotatable central electrode mount and interchangeable carriers for different head shapes.

#VR#AffectiveComputing

Passenger emotion assessment in virtual driving

BCI Lab, Academia Sinica
Sep–Dec 2025

I developed a Unity-based VR driving testbed to examine how driving styles and road environments affect passenger valence and arousal. My work covered the environment, randomized driving events, and pilot experiments using EEG and self-reports. Further data collection is on hold pending a reliable EEG-based emotion recognition model.

#VR#MedicalTraining

Interactive cardiac catheterization training

Advanced Medical Device Lab, NTU
Jul 2023–Aug 2024

I developed a Unity-based VR prototype for interactive guidewire positioning and balloon-driven stent expansion. This work explored interaction for training; it was an early prototype.
