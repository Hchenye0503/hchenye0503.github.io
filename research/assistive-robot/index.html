Multimodal assistive robot

Combining brain, muscle, and gesture inputs for a mobile robot that retrieves objects.

BCI Lab, Academia Sinica
Dr. Yu-Te Wang
Jul–Aug 2025
Conference paper, BCI 2026
Research questionMy contributionOutcomePublication

Research question

Moving a robot through a space and manipulating an object involve different control demands. Our team developed a mobile assistive robot that combines EEG, EMG, and vision-based gesture inputs for these tasks.

My contribution

My work

  • Designed and 3D-printed the mobile base.
  • Implemented MediaPipe-based gesture control for an open-source six-degree-of-freedom arm.
  • Used gestures to select joints and lateral hand motion to control their rotation.

Team system

The integrated platform combined these components with EEG and EMG control. Motor-imagery and SSVEP decoding were developed by other team members.

Outcome

The integrated team system completed two object-retrieval tasks. The work was reported in From Movement to Manipulation: Multimodal Control of a Mobile Assistive Robot at BCI 2026.

My contribution brought mechanical design and a gesture-based control interface into a broader BCI system.
