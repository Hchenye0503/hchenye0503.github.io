RehabVerse

Investigating motor engagement and score feedback in multiplayer VR rehabilitation.

BCI Lab, Academia Sinica
Dr. Yu-Te Wang
Jun–Sep 2026
Submitted to ACM CHI 2027
Research questionMy contributionAnalysis approachCurrent status

Research question

How can score feedback support engagement during VR motor training? RehabVerse provides a shared virtual environment for executed and imagined movements, with scoring informed by muscle and brain signals.

My work focused on motor-related EEG activity and responses following score adjustments.

My contribution

My work

  • Co-developed the EEG analysis strategy with collaborators.
  • Interpreted motor-related EEG activity and stage-based lateralization analyses.
  • Led manuscript writing and figure preparation.

Team contributions

  • Developed the multiplayer VR game and scoring system.
  • Collected the data for this study.
  • Implemented the analysis code.

Analysis approach

We examined mu-band event-related desynchronization during executed and imagined movements, alongside stage-based analyses of EEG lateralization following score boosts.

This work connects a question about interactive feedback with physiological measurements of motor-related activity. It is an exploratory study of the platform.

Current status

The manuscript, RehabVerse: A Virtual-Reality Multiplayer Rehabilitation Game Scored from Muscle and Brain, with Adaptive Score Manipulation, has been submitted to ACM CHI 2027.

A separate study examines human and AI competition using the same platform. My role in that study is described under additional research.
