Chen-Ye Hong

I’m a research assistant in the BCI Lab at Academia Sinica, working with Dr. Yu-Te Wang. I hold a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from National Taiwan University.

My research interests are brain–computer interfaces and human–robot interaction, with an interest in using neural, physiological, and behavioral signals to help interactive systems adapt to users’ intentions and states. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

#LLM#ReinforcementLearning

LLM-driven robot pet

I developed a MuJoCo robot pet that combines VLM-derived human behavioral cues with simulated internal states to guide LLM-based behavior selection. I also designed speed-dependent rewards for PPO locomotion and evaluated velocity tracking and stability across three training seeds.
